# LinkedIn Post Optimization Framework

When transforming user drafts into LinkedIn posts:

## 1. Pattern-Interrupt Hook
The first 2 lines determine whether readers click "see more".
- Start with a bold assertion, surprising statistic, counter-intuitive insight, or clear value promise.
- Avoid generic greetings ("Hope everyone is having a great week!").

## 2. Visual Readability & Formatting
- Use single-sentence paragraphs separated by blank line breaks.
- Use numbered lists or clean emojis (1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣) for key takeaways.
- Keep sentences short, punchy, and human. Avoid academic jargon or AI corporate speak.

## 3. Credibility & Storytelling
- Weave personal observation, direct experience, or concrete metrics into the core message.
- Explain *why* the insight matters to professionals in the domain.

## 4. Engagement Call to Action (CTA)
- End with a low-friction question inviting comments (e.g. "What's your biggest takeaway?", "How does your team handle this?").
- Include 3 to 5 targeted, highly relevant hashtags at the bottom.
