# LinkedIn Hook Optimization Framework

## Essential Elements
1. **The Hook (First 2 Lines)**:
   - Must stop scrolling before the `...see more` fold.
   - Use contrast, curiosity, vulnerability, counter-intuitive statements, or bold metrics.
   - Example: `I got rejected 40 times before landing Google. Here is the exact template I used.`
2. **Visual Formatting**:
   - One line per sentence for high mobile readability.
   - Use whitespace effectively; avoid wall-of-text blocks.
3. **Core Story / Value**:
   - Provide concrete actionable advice, personal insight, or industry takeaways.
4. **Call to Action (CTA)**:
   - Ask an engaging open-ended question to drive meaningful comments and algorithmic distribution.
