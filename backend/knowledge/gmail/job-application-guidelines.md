# Job Application Email Guidelines

## Core Structure
1. **Subject Line**: Specific, professional, and clear (e.g. `Application: [Role Title] - [Applicant Name]`).
2. **Greeting**: Direct, professional greeting addressed to hiring manager or recruiter.
3. **Opening Hook**: State the role applied for, enthusiasm, and key value proposition in the first 2 sentences.
4. **Body Paragraphs**: 
   - Highlight 2-3 relevant accomplishments aligned with job requirements.
   - Quantify impact where possible (e.g. `Increased performance by 35%`).
5. **Call to Action**: Polite request for an interview or conversation.
6. **Closing & Signature**: Professional sign-off (`Sincerely`, `Best regards`) with contact info.
