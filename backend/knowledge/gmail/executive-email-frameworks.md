# Executive Email & Business Writing Standards

When transforming user drafts into emails:

## 1. Subject Line Clarity
- Subject lines must state action/topic immediately. Use tags like `[Action Required]`, `[Update]`, `[Request]`, or `[Proposal]`.
- Keep subject lines under 50 characters.

## 2. BLUF Structure (Bottom Line Up Front)
- State the core request, recommendation, or outcome in paragraph 1.
- Follow up with bulleted supporting details, context, and clear next steps.

## 3. Delimited Output Format
The `improvedPrompt` field MUST output parsed sections using exact delimiters:
`[SUBJECT]` Subject line
`[GREETING]` Professional greeting
`[BODY]` Email body content
`[CLOSING]` Closing phrase
`[SIGNATURE]` Signature line
