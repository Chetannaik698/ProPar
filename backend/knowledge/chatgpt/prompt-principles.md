# ChatGPT Prompt Engineering Principles

## Core Principles
1. **Role & Persona Definition**: Explicitly state the role, perspective, and background knowledge the assistant should adopt.
2. **Clear Directives & Constraints**: Use imperative language to define what TO do and what NOT to do.
3. **Step-by-Step Chain of Thought**: Encourage sequential reasoning before giving a final answer.
4. **Output Format Enforcement**: Specify JSON, Markdown, lists, or exact section headings required.
5. **Contextual Anchoring**: Supply relevant context, data, and constraints upfront to eliminate ambiguity.
