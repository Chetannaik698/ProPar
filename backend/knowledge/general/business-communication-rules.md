# Business Communication & Reasoning Principles

## Frameworks
1. **Pyramid Principle**: State the main recommendation or conclusion first, followed by supporting arguments and grouped data.
2. **MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive)**: Ensure options and considerations cover the entire scope without overlapping.
3. **Problem-Solution-Impact**: Clearly articulate the underlying problem, proposed intervention, and expected outcome.
4. **BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front)**: Deliver critical conclusions at the beginning of communications.
